Whether you’re an undergraduate building your first full-stack prototype or a graduate student training specialized neural networks, mentors and cloud compute grants are ready to support your ambition.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a Computer Science major to participate? expand_more

Not at all! We actively encourage designers, product thinkers, business majors, and engineers of any discipline. Hackathons thrive when cross-functional perspectives merge.

Can I stay overnight in the Engineering Hall? expand_more

Yes, designated sleeping classrooms and quiet zones will be available with campus security present throughout both nights. Please bring a sleeping bag if you intend to rest on-site.

What are the track themes and hardware perks? expand_more

We feature Web3 & Applied AI, Mobile Systems, and Social Good & Climate. Our hardware checkout includes Raspberry Pi 5 kits, ESP32 boards, VR headsets, and soldering workstations.
